# Publish the legal pages

`ailv-app-legal-pages` (Publish the legal pages): Publish a privacy policy, terms of sale, and refund-or-returns policy before the first order, in plain language the buyer's own. Name every subprocessor (payments, email, hosting, analytics, CDN), what is kept, how long, and the contact for deletion requests. Show total price, one-time versus recurring, refund terms, and what is delivered when, at the point of purchase — never only in the fine print. State plainly what is not warranted and cap liability at what was paid. The pages state they are not legal advice, and the owner gets local review before launch.

## Checks

- privacy, terms, and refund-or-returns pages live before first order
- subprocessors, retention, and deletion contact named
- price, cadence, refunds, and delivery shown at purchase
- pages disclaim legal advice; owner review recorded
